Waiting usually pays. Except when it doesn't.

Economists who study resale markets have found that prices for the same seats tend to fall as game day gets closer, because sellers would rather take less than end up with a ticket nobody can use.

But it isn't a rule. A star returns from injury, a rivalry heats up, or the cheap seats sell out, and prices jump. Tixcast tracks every game across the league so it can tell the two apart.

Questions

Does Tixcast sell tickets?
No. It tells you when to buy, then sends you to marketplaces like SeatGeek and StubHub to check out. We may earn a small referral fee when you do, at no cost to you.
Which games does it cover?
We track every NBA game. The beta starts with Lakers and Clippers home games, with more teams soon after.
Is it free?
Yes, during the beta. Waitlist members get in first.
How accurate is it?
We'll publish how our calls perform against simply waiting until game day, so you can judge for yourself. Every forecast shows its odds, not just a verdict.
